## How this tool handles your image

Click the background you want to remove. That click clears only the connected pixels close to the color you hit, and the preview shows them as transparent on a checkerboard. The file stays in this browser. Saving a pass does not upload it or store it.

## Default result

Opening a file changes nothing. You can download the original pixels as a PNG before you click. The first click is a preview of one area. It is not kept until you save that pass.

## How to use it

Drop a logo or image. Click the outer background. If the edges look right, choose **Save and make another area transparent**. Then click any patch that is still filled, including the holes inside letters such as O, A, and B. Those holes are separate from the outer background, so the first click cannot reach them. Repeat until the checkerboard shows through every area you wanted removed, then download the PNG.

Undo drops the last saved pass. A click before you save only replaces the current preview. If a click removes part of the subject, undo and click closer to the edge of the background.

Pair it with [crop image](/crop-image) if you want a tighter frame, or [convert image](/convert-image) if you later need another format. Converting to JPEG will fill the transparent pixels again.

## Limits

Inputs are limited to 25 MB and 8192 pixels per side. GIF files use the first frame. Everything runs on your device.
