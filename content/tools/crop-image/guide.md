## How this tool handles your image

Crop lets you draw a rectangle over your photo and export only that region. The image is decoded and painted on a canvas locally—no upload step. When you first open a file, the selection covers the full frame so you can download unchanged if you only needed a quick look.

## Default result

Until you move the handles, the crop matches the original width and height. Picking a ratio chip locks proportions (1:1 square, 4:5 portrait, 16:9 widescreen) while you drag corners or edges.

The exported file uses the `cropped` suffix in its name.

## How to use it

Drop or select your image. Drag the overlay to the area you want, or tap a ratio preset for social layouts. Download when the preview matches what you need.

Pair with [rotate image](/rotate-image) if the photo is sideways, or [resize image](/resize-image) after cropping to hit a pixel target.

## Limits

Inputs are limited to 25 MB and 8192 pixels per side. Everything runs in the browser on your machine.
