## How this tool handles your image

LinkedIn link posts and article cards often expect a **1200×627** preview image—one pixel shorter than Facebook’s 630-tall spec, but close enough that many teams keep separate masters. This page applies that LinkedIn frame with a cover-and-crop pass entirely in your browser.

## Default result

The default Link image export is **1200×627** pixels. Filenames include the `linkedin` suffix.

If you also publish to Facebook, note the one-pixel height difference versus **1200×630** on our [Facebook resize](/resize-image-for-facebook) page so you export the right file per network.

## How to use it

Drop or select your photo or graphic. Preview the crop, then download and upload through LinkedIn’s post composer or your scheduling tool.

Combine with [crop image](/crop-image) when you need to tighten composition before hitting the preset, or [compress image](/compress-image) if the PNG is heavy for email attachments.

## Limits

Files must be at most 25 MB with no dimension above 8192 pixels. No server upload—LinkedIn receives the file only when you upload it there.
