## How the conversion works

Choose the unit you have, type the amount, and choose the unit you want. The result updates as you type. Fifteen shotangsho is 9.075 katha.

The factors are the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics survey table. One shotangsho (also called a decimal or shotok) is 435.6 square feet. One katha is 720 square feet, which is 1.6528926 shotangsho. One bigha is 20 katha, or 14,400 square feet. One acre is 100 shotangsho, 60.5 katha, and 3.025 bigha. One ojutangsho is one hundredth of a shotangsho. One chhatak is one sixteenth of a katha, or 45 square feet.

## What this does not convert

A local bigha can be larger or smaller than 20 katha. Kani, gonda, kora, and kranti are left out: some records use 120 shotangsho to a kani, and others use about 40. Check the deed or khatian before you rely on a district unit.

## How to use it

Set From to Shotangsho, type 15, and set To to Katha. Use Swap to reverse the two units. Square feet, square yards, square metres, and hectares use the same survey acre, so a chain of conversions comes back to the amount you started with.
