## How this tool handles your image

Link previews and shared URLs on Facebook commonly use a **1200×630** open-graph image. This preset covers that 1.91:1 frame with your artwork, center-crops, and exports exact pixels—all in the browser without sending the photo to Facebook or to us.

## Default result

The Link image preset produces **1200×630** pixels, matching the dimensions Facebook’s sharing debugger and most documentation cite for og:image assets.

Downloaded files use the `facebook` suffix so they are distinct from your master creative.

## How to use it

Add your source image. Check that logos and headlines sit comfortably in the center third; cover-crop trims the sides on wide photos. Save the result and paste the file into your CMS, meta tags, or Facebook’s sharing tools.

For a slightly different social spec, compare [LinkedIn link images at 1200×627](/resize-image-for-linkedin). For arbitrary sizes, use [resize image](/resize-image).

## Limits

25 MB maximum file size and 8192 pixels on the longest edge. Processing stays on your computer.
