## How this tool handles your image

Instagram posts look best when pixels match what the app expects. This preset scales your photo to cover the chosen frame, then center-crops so nothing is letterboxed. The entire workflow runs in your browser—your camera roll stays on your device.

## Default result

The Feed chip is selected first and exports **1080×1350**, the 4:5 portrait size that fills a standard Instagram feed slot without unexpected cropping in the app.

Switch to **Story** for **1080×1920** (9:16) when you are preparing a full-screen story or reel cover. Both sizes are on this page; you do not need a separate URL for story work.

## How to use it

Drop or browse to your image. Confirm the Feed or Story chip, check the preview, and download. The filename ends with `instagram`.

For free-form dimensions or a different aspect ratio before posting, use [crop image](/crop-image) with the 4:5 chip or the general [resize image](/resize-image) tool.

## Limits

Maximum input is 25 MB and 8192 pixels on the longest side. Processing is local; nothing is uploaded to Instagram or to us.
