## How this tool handles your image

YouTube thumbnails display at **1280×720** (16:9) in the upload dialog and across most surfaces. This tool covers that frame with your art, then center-crops to exact pixels so text and faces stay centered. All rendering happens in the browser; the video file never touches our servers because you are only preparing a still image.

## Default result

Every download from this page is **1280×720** pixels—the size YouTube documents for custom thumbnails. The `youtube` suffix is added to the filename so exports are easy to spot in your downloads folder.

## How to use it

Load a screenshot, photo, or graphic. Adjust composition mentally against the preview—anything near the edges may be clipped by the cover step. Download and upload the PNG or JPEG in YouTube Studio.

Need a wider banner or a square icon? Use [resize image](/resize-image) or [crop image](/crop-image) with a 16:9 ratio first, then return here for the final pixel lock.

## Limits

Inputs up to 25 MB and 8192 pixels per edge are accepted. Larger files are blocked before processing. No upload to YouTube occurs from this site—you save locally and upload in Studio yourself.
