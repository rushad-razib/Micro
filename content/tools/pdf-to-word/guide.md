## How PDF to Word works here

PDF.js reads the text layer of your PDF in the browser. That text is written into a `.docx` with the docx library. Nothing is uploaded.

## What to expect

Digital PDFs exported from Word or similar apps usually have a text layer and convert into editable paragraphs. Image-only scans do not: there is no OCR in this release. Layout will be simpler than the original PDF.

## How to use it

Drop a PDF. If text is found, download the DOCX. To create a PDF from Word again, use [Word to PDF](/word-to-pdf).

## Limits

About 25 MB and 100 pages. Password-protected PDFs are not supported.
