## What can be in a photo

Stub. Camera make, date, software, and sometimes a location.

## Why it matters

Stub. A shared original can show more than the picture.

## How to remove it here

Use [strip image metadata](/strip-image-metadata). GPS is described as location embedded, not as a street address.

## What this site does not do

The file stays in your browser. We do not store EXIF.
