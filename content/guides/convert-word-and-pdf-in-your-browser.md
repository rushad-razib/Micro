## The privacy trade-off

Server converters can chase perfect layout because they run Word or LibreOffice remotely. That means your file leaves the device. This site keeps [Word to PDF](/word-to-pdf) and [PDF to Word](/pdf-to-word) in the browser instead, with honest limits.

## Word to PDF

Use a `.docx` with mostly body text. You get a readable PDF without uploading. Expect imperfect match on complex tables, text boxes, and brand fonts. Old `.doc` files must be saved as DOCX first.

## PDF to Word

The PDF needs a text layer. Exports from Word usually work; phone scans of paper do not until OCR exists elsewhere. The DOCX is for editing paragraphs, not cloning magazine layouts.

## When to use other PDF tools

Merge, split, rotate, and images-to-PDF stay on [PDF tools](/documents). They do not replace Office conversion—they prepare or finish the file around it.
