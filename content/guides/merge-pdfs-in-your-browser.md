## Why merge locally

Many online merge sites upload your files to a server. If the PDF holds contracts or IDs, that is an unnecessary risk. The [merge PDF](/merge-pdf) tool on this site builds one document in your browser and only downloads when you choose.

## Order and ranges

Files join in the order you add them. If you only need part of a source, [split PDF](/split-pdf) to the pages you want, then merge those pieces. [Rotate PDF](/rotate-pdf) helps when a scan is sideways before you combine.

## Limits to remember

Large or encrypted PDFs may be rejected. Stay within the size and page caps shown on the tool page, and keep an original copy of important files.
